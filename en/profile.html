Hiroshi SUGITA

  • Professor, Faculty of Human Development, Kokugakuin University
  • Former School Inspector, MEXT
  • Board Member and Member of the International Exchange Committee, Japanese Society for the Study of Special Activities
  • Advisor, National Association for Special Activities Research (Japan)
  • Program committee member, NHK educational TV

Career

  1. 1980 – 1998Elementary school teacher, Urawa City, Saitama (4 schools, 18 years)
  2. 1998 – 2001Supervisor, Urawa City Board of Education
  3. 2001 – 2004Chief Supervisor, Saitama City Board of Education
  4. 2004 – 2013Senior Specialist for Curriculum, Curriculum Division, Elementary and Secondary Education Bureau, MEXT; and Curriculum Researcher, National Institute for Educational Policy Research (NIER)
  5. 2013 – 2015School Inspector, Elementary and Secondary Education Bureau, MEXT
  6. 2015 –Professor, Faculty of Human Development, Kokugakuin University
  7. 2026Founded the Hiroshi Sugita TOKKATSU Education Institute

Selected work

  • Contributed to the official commentary on special activities in the national Course of Study (1998, 2008), and chaired the committee for the 2017 edition
  • Member, Working Group on Special Activities, Central Council for Education (2015 – 2016)
  • Chair of several MEXT / NIER research committees on special activities, assessment, and teaching videos (2017 – 2023)
  • JICA expert for the introduction of TOKKATSU in Egypt (2016 – )
  • Appearances on NHK “Close-up Gendai” (2023) and in the national outreach for the film “The Making of a Japanese” (2024)

Books

  • よりよい人間関係を築く特別活動Special Activities for Building Better RelationshipsTosho Bunka, 2009
  • 特別活動の教育技術Teaching Skills for Special ActivitiesShogakukan, 2013
  • 特別活動で、日本の教育が変わる！Special Activities Can Change Japanese Education (co-author)Shogakukan, 2020

Author of three books, and editor or co-author of about fifteen more (in Japanese).

Lectures and training

The Institute welcomes requests for school-based training, lectures, lesson guidance and support for research schools — in Japan and overseas. The request form is in Japanese; for requests in English, please write in English in the form.
