Three pillars

Field

Field-based practice

The Institute works alongside schools on the real challenges they face — preventing problems before they arise and building classes that are ready to learn — and deepens practical wisdom grounded in sound theory.

Children

Child-centered dialogue and collaboration

The Institute values the processes through which children build their groups and themselves by reaching consensus and making decisions together, and supports schools where every child shines as someone of value.

World

Connecting the global and the local

The Institute shares the strengths of Japanese TOKKATSU with the world, and proposes flexible learning that fits the context of each region, in Japan and abroad.

What is TOKKATSU?

TOKKATSU (特別活動, “special activities”) is the part of Japanese schooling where children run class meetings, student councils, clubs and school events themselves. Through talking, deciding and acting together, they learn to build a better group — and to grow as individuals within it.

The approach has drawn international attention, and has been introduced in countries such as Egypt and Mongolia.
